## References and dispositions

### BT17-FILLER
- Source: 2017_Beartracks.pdf, PDF p14 / Q2 p6, Mark Richardson: Locating the Fuel Tank Filler Hole
- Model / topic: 4-Place scratch build / WING_FUEL
- Use: Technique reference. Transfer the actual fitted filler-neck center to a paper template; fit the actual tank before marking. Historical hole sizes and cutting methods are not Companion instructions. Tank-bay bottom drain openings are a different operation.

### BT18-RIGGING
- Source: 2018_Beartracks.pdf, PDF pp19-21 / Q3 pp3-5, Kevin Deutscher: Rigging a Bearhawk, Part 1
- Model / topic: Original 4-Place / CONTROLS / EMPENNAGE
- Use: Qualitative checks adopted. Check free movement at each connection stage, hose/spring clearance, hinge alignment and independent final control-direction/security checks. Numerical incidence, shim fabrication and balance changes are not adopted.
